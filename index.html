The flow

From a job listing to a first day — seen from both sides.

The candidate and the employer each have their own sequence. Four moments are shared: the two tracks meet, and the same application carries the state forward.

Three career families, ranked, each with the reasoning behind it.

03

Live listings inside those fields, with the skills each one asks for.

04

Written for that listing and kept, so the next application starts ahead.

Application submitted The candidate applies with the tailored resume. The employer receives it scored on skills, experience, interest fit and written answers.

Interview scheduled The prep pack unlocks for the candidate. If the time does not work, they propose another and the employer answers in the same thread.

For both sides

Neither side is doing the other's work.

Job seeker

Applications that argue their own case.

  • A ranked, explained view of which fields fit, and why.
  • A resume written for each listing, kept and reusable.
  • Every application's real status, not silence after submitting.
  • Interview details and prep material from the company itself.

Employer

A shortlist instead of an inbox.

  • Applicants scored on skills, experience, fit and written answers.
  • Screening answered before the first call, not during it.
  • Listings shown to candidates whose interests match the field.
  • Review state recorded from what you actually did.
